Crafting Sweet Memories Since 2009

Welcome to Frosty Delights, where every scoop tells a story of passion, quality, and pure joy. Founded in 2009 by the Sharma family, we started as a small parlour with just 5 flavors and a big dream.

Today, we proudly serve over 50 handcrafted flavors made from the freshest local ingredients, exotic fruits, and premium Belgian chocolate. Our recipes are a blend of traditional Indian flavors and international inspirations.

Every batch is made fresh daily in our kitchen, ensuring you always get the creamiest, most delicious ice cream possible. We believe happiness comes in scoops!
